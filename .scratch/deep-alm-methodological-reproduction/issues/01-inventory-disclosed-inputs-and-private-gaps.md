# Inventory the paper's disclosed inputs and proprietary gaps

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

For the no-swap 5-year and 15-year Deep ALM settings, which initial values, cash-flow ladders, behavioral assumptions, market parameters, regulatory parameters, and training inputs are explicitly disclosed in the paper or its cited public sources, and which depend on unavailable bank data? Produce a source-cited inventory that distinguishes directly stated values, derivable values, and genuinely missing values, so later decisions can construct the Reference Bank without accidental invention.

## Answer

Public SNB data and the paper are sufficient for a no-swap methodological reproduction, but not for the paper's original numerical results. The unavailable inputs include the initial 180-month cash-flow ladders, absolute bank scale, product maturity allocations, loan spread, and operating-cost bases. The research also separates proprietary gaps from implementation details that the paper leaves unstated or ambiguous.
