# Catalogue no-swap benchmarks, experiments, and validation claims

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

Which benchmark strategies, model variants, 5-year and 15-year experiments, loss settings, constraints, metrics, figures, tables, and qualitative claims belong to the paper's no-swap scope? Produce a source-cited catalogue that separates required reproduction targets from swap-only or otherwise excluded material and records where the paper reports only aggregated or relative results.

## Answer

Required no-swap scope is BM^E, BM^C, BM^D, and MM at 5-year and 15-year horizons, plus MM(15y|5y). Training uses the asymmetric target loss together with a constraint penalty; CRRA is an evaluation metric only. Published tables are directional validation landmarks rather than equality targets. The detailed catalogue also separates swap-only material and records cross-reference inconsistencies in the paper.

See [the research report](../research/no-swap-experiments.md).
